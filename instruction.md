The compliance return for bore PB-07 is due and the abstraction licence wants the water level for every day of the abstraction period, the depth to water on the day the licence names, and whether the bore is inside its licence. The logger export and the licence settings are in `/app/data`, with a README describing each file, what the readings carry, and how the last return was put together.

The hydro team's tools are Java. `/app/bore` is a project with a stub at `/app/bore/src/Drawdown.java` and a build script, `/app/bore/build.sh`, which is there if you want it; we don't run it. The requirements:

- `java -cp /app/bore/out Drawdown <data-dir> <output-json>` writes one JSON object with exactly five keys and nothing else: `daily_depth_m`, an object with one entry per calendar day from the date of `abstraction_start` to the calendar date of the last reading in `levels.csv` inclusive, keyed by the date as `YYYY-MM-DD`, giving the mean depth to water over that day. The last of those days is keyed even when its 24 hours run past the last reading; `projected_depth_m`, the mean depth to water over the day of abstraction that `projection_day` names; `drawdown_mm_per_day`, the drawdown rate below; `tidal_period_h`, the period you read off that bore's `commissioning_10min.csv`, in hours; and `within_licence`, true or false
- the drawdown rate is the fall from the mean on the day abstraction started to the projected depth, in mm, divided by `projection_day`. `within_licence` is true when that rate is at or under `licence_limit_mm_per_day` in the `bore.json` of the directory you were given and the projected depth is shallower than `pump_intake_depth_m`
- the readings carry the tidal term the README describes, a sinusoid at the period that bore breathes at, evaluated at each reading's own time, sitting on top of the level the licence is about, which is flat before `abstraction_start` and falls at a steady rate after it for as long as the abstraction runs, so the level on the day `projection_day` names is that same steady fall carried out to that day; the period differs from bore to bore, sits between 10 and 15 hours on all of them as the README says, and is there to be read off that bore's `commissioning_10min.csv`, and how you fit the tidal term and the rate of fall from the readings is your call, including which readings you use
- the box is offline and no outside library is on the compile path, so the standard library that ships with Java is all you have; we compile `src/*.java` with javac ourselves, every file you leave under `src`, and run the class from `/app/bore` with nothing on the box executable but java itself while it runs
- the same class is run unchanged on the exports from other bores, the same three files and README in the same format, with their own dates and licence settings, so it works from the directory it is given rather than from anything written into the source. Every compliance record is logged on a fixed interval, twelve or twenty four hours, and that interval is never a whole multiple of the period the bore breathes at
- the files in `/app/data` stay as shipped, they are checked against our copy

We check against the licence check as it was done: the contractor's 10 minute logger went back on the bore for the abstraction period and for the day the licence names, and the regulator takes each day's mean over the 24 hours that begin at the time of day the abstraction started, so the day keyed 2025-08-04 is the 24 hours from `abstraction_start` on that date. A day is not a whole number of tidal cycles, so that mean is not the same as the level with the tide taken out. Every day's level has to be within 0.03 m of that day's mean, the projected depth within 0.10 m of the mean on the day the licence names, and the verdict has to agree, on every bore it is run against. The rate you report has to be within the same slack those two depth bars leave it, which is 0.13 m spread over `projection_day` days. No bore sits near either licence limit either: on the regulator's own record every one is clear of `licence_limit_mm_per_day` by more than that slack, and its projected depth is at least 0.4 m clear of `pump_intake_depth_m`, so a report inside the bars cannot land on the wrong side of the licence and nothing turns on how a figure exactly equal to either limit is treated. The period you report has to be within 0.15 h of the one that bore's commissioning dump carries.
